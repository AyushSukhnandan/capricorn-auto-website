Our Story

Excellence by
Design

Founded in 2016, backed by 37+ years of combined automotive experience, and based in Mount Edgecombe, KwaZulu-Natal, Capricorn Auto is an independent German vehicle specialist. Built on more than 37 years of combined hands-on automotive expertise, every vehicle is treated with the precision, technical excellence, and attention it deserves.

While premium German engineering remains our speciality—including BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Volkswagen, Porsche and Mini—we proudly service and maintain all vehicle makes and models. Supported by an extensive network of specialised technical consultants and trusted OEM parts suppliers, we deliver dealer-level diagnostics, routine servicing, mechanical repairs, and performance upgrades.

Whether serving private owners, fleet operators, insurance providers, or mechanical warranty companies, our goal remains the same: to provide fair, professional, efficient, and dependable automotive solutions with uncompromising quality. As a proud Level 1 B-BBEE Contributor, Capricorn Auto continues to build lasting relationships through honesty, transparency, and service you can rely on.
